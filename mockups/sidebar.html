v0.2.0
  • Groups. Input (what the controller sends), Output (where it goes: MIDI, OSC, later a virtual gamepad and keyboard/mouse, dimmed here as placeholders), Tools. About sits at the bottom. A new tab is one entry in the right group, with no fight for width.
  • Widths. Labels are 208 px, icons 68 px. Auto switches to icons below 1100 px, so at the 960 px minimum the content gets 892 px (today's full-width layout gets 960). Collapse lets you pick icons at any size, and the app would remember it.
  • Top bar. Only the tab title and the live status are left: replay, device, Hz, battery, record and power. They have room now, even with a recording and a replay running at 960 px.
  • Live dots. A green dot when an output is sending (MIDI port open, OSC streaming) and a red one on Recorder while recording, so you see it from any tab. In icon mode they sit on the icon's corner, and hovering shows the name.
